From an algorithmic and project controls perspective:
1. Explain exactly what breaks in a 3,000-activity solar power project when dates are treated as independent, manually edited inputs rather than computed values.

## 🏗️ Section B: Real-World EPC & Construction Scenarios

### Question 4: The Delayed Inverter Delivery
In our 100MW Rajasthan Solar IPP project, consider the following chain of activities:
* **Activity A:** Foundation Civil Pours (Duration: 10 days)
* **Activity B:** 7-Day Concrete Curing (Lag: 7 days)
* **Activity C:** Inverter Station Equipment Erection (Duration: 5 days)
* **Activity D:** DC Cable Termination & Inverter Commissioning (Duration: 6 days)

Suppose the site team experiences a sudden cement shortage that pushes the completion of Activity A from Day 10 to Day 25 (a 15-day delay).
1. If the planner is using a **date-driven tool** without automatic cascade, what manual steps must they perform, and what risks are introduced?
2. In **Primavera P6**, assuming proper Finish-to-Start relationships and lags are in place, what exact steps does the planner take, and what happens to Activities B, C, and D?
3. What happens if Activity C had a hard constraint (`Must Finish On Day 22`) applied by an inexperienced planner?
