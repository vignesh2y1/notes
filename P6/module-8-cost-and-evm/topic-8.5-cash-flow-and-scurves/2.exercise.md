## 🧠 Section A: Conceptual & Mathematical Foundations

### Question 1: The Calculus of the S-Curve (Derivative & Inflection Point)

1. What does the first derivative $\frac{dS(t)}{dt}$ represent physically and commercially?
2. What does the second derivative $\frac{d^2S(t)}{dt^2} = 0$ represent?
3. Explain why construction contractors experience their highest statistical frequency of cash-flow distress and bankruptcy in the immediate vicinity of the inflection point $t_{\text{inflect}}$.

### Question 2: Derivation of the "Banana Curve" Envelope

2. Prove mathematically that for any non-negative cost distribution, $PV_{\text{Early}}(t) \ge PV_{\text{Late}}(t)$ for all $t \in [0, T]$.
3. Under what structural condition does $PV_{\text{Early}}(t) = PV_{\text{Late}}(t)$ across the entire project duration?
