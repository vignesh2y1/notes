3. If a solar substation was planned to take 8 months to build, but actually takes 12 months to reach completion, calculate both classical $SPI$ and time-based $SPI_t$ on the day the substation is commissioned.

### Question 3: The 4 Performance Quadrants
Consider the Cartesian plane divided by $CPI = 1.00$ and $SPI = 1.00$:
1. Sketch an ASCII diagram labeling Quadrant 1, Quadrant 2, Quadrant 3, and Quadrant 4.

   - **Quadrant 2** ($CPI < 1.0, SPI > 1.0$): Why is the project ahead of schedule but over budget?
   - **Quadrant 3** ($CPI > 1.0, SPI < 1.0$): Why is this known as the "False Comfort" quadrant?
3. In a lump-sum Turnkey EPC contract with heavy delay Liquidated Damages (₹5,00,000/day), why might a contractor deliberately steer a project from Quadrant 1 into Quadrant 2?
