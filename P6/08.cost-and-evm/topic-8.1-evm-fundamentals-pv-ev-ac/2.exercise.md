Independent site inspection reveals that out of 32 Inverter Stations planned to be completed, only 8 have been constructed, and only 40,000 out of 160,000 planned tracker piles have been driven into the ground.
1. Explain the fundamental flaw in the Contractor PM's claim using the Earned Value mental model.
2. If the physical work completed has a baseline budgeted value ($EV$) of ₹65 Crore, calculate the actual cost overrun or underrun and schedule slippage in currency terms.
