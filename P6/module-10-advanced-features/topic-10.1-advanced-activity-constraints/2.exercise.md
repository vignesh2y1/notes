## 🧠 Section A: Conceptual & Mathematical Foundations

### Question 1: Soft vs. Hard Constraint Topological Invariants
1. Define the fundamental mathematical difference between a soft constraint (such as *Finish On or Before*) and a hard constraint (such as *Mandatory Finish*) during the forward pass and backward pass.
2. Given a two-activity network $A \xrightarrow{FS=0} B$ where $D_A = 10\text{d}$ and $D_B = 5\text{d}$:
   - Suppose Activity B is assigned a soft constraint `Finish On or Before Day 12`.
   - Now suppose Activity B is assigned a hard constraint `Mandatory Finish Day 12`.
   Calculate Early Start, Early Finish, Late Start, Late Finish, and Total Float for both activities under both cases. Explain why the resulting float behavior differs fundamentally.
