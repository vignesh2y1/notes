### Question 5: Surya 100MW Solar IPP — Contemporaneous Time Impact Analysis (TIA)
On 01-August-2026 (Update 06, Data Date = Day 120), the project is exactly on schedule ($TF = 0$, Projected Finish = Day 240).
The state electrical inspectorate (CEA) issues a stop-work notice on the 220kV Pooling Substation due to an external grid fault study review. The delay lasts 25 calendar days.
- The contractor prepares a fragnet consisting of 3 activities:
  - $FRAG\text{-}01$ (CEA Notification Review, $D = 5\text{d}$)
  - $FRAG\text{-}02$ (Grid Fault Study Re-simulation, $D = 15\text{d}$)
  - $FRAG\text{-}03$ (Discom Safety Endorsement, $D = 5\text{d}$)
- The fragnet predecessor is $ACT\text{-}SUB$ (Substation Civil Handover, $EF = \text{Day } 130$).
- The fragnet successor is $ACT\text{-}ENERG$ (Plant Energization & Synchronous Testing, Pre-impact $ES = \text{Day } 135$).
1. Trace the step-by-step TIA procedure to measure the net project impact.
2. What was the pre-impact Free Float between $ACT\text{-}SUB$ and $ACT\text{-}ENERG$?
3. How many calendar days of contractual **Extension of Time (EOT)** is Surya Renewables legally entitled to claim from the owner?

### Question 6: Concurrent Delay Apportionment & The Pacing Defense

- **Delay Event A:** The owner fails to deliver the owner-furnished 220kV Main Power Transformer for 30 days (Compensable delay on Path A).
- **Delay Event B:** The contractor experiences severe labor absenteeism on inverter foundation casting for 20 days (Non-Excusable delay on Path B).
Both Path A and Path B are independent parallel paths merging into Commissioning.
1. How should the 30-day delay window be legally apportioned between Days 1–20 and Days 21–30?
2. Under standard construction contract law, is the contractor entitled to:
   - Time extension for Days 1–20?
   - Extended site overhead compensation for Days 1–20?
   - Time extension for Days 21–30?
   - Extended site overhead compensation for Days 21–30?
3. What evidentiary burden must the contractor satisfy to legally mount a **Pacing Defense** for Event B?
