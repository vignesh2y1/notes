## 🧠 Section A: Conceptual & Architectural Principles

### Question 1: The Named-Individual Trap vs Generic Role Pools

1. Explain three critical business and project controls risks of this practice.
2. How does the separation of **Roles** (capability demand) and **Resources** (capacity supply) solve these risks?
3. Contrast this distinction with the software engineering concept of Interfaces vs Concrete Structs in Go.

### Question 2: The Three Resource Types & Engine Invariants

1. State the default Unit of Measure (UOM) for Labor and Non-Labor, and explain why Material requires a user-defined UOM.

### Question 4: Calendar Interplay — Task Dependent vs Resource Dependent
Consider Activity `ACT-INV-04` (*"Central Inverter 2.5MW Commissioning"*):
* Activity Calendar: 6-Day Work Week (Mon–Sat, 8 hrs/day).
* Assigned Resource: Arun Kumar.
* Resource Calendar: 5-Day Work Week (Mon–Fri, 8 hrs/day).
* Planned Duration: 16 working hours (2 days).
* Early Start: Friday at 08:00 AM.
1. If the activity type is set to **Task Dependent** (`TT_Task`), calculate the Early Finish date and time. Does Arun Kumar work on Saturday? What anomaly is flagged?
2. If the activity type is set to **Resource Dependent** (`TT_Rsrc`), calculate the Early Finish date and time. How many calendar days elapse?
3. Under what specific contractual conditions should an EPC planner mandate `TT_Rsrc` over `TT_Task`?
