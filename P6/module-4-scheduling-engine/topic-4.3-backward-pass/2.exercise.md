### Question 1: Algorithmic Necessity of Reverse Topological Order

1. Prove why the Backward Pass **must** process nodes in the exact reverse of the topological order ($\text{reverse}(\text{TopoOrder})$). What mathematical invariant would be violated if nodes were processed in arbitrary order?
2. Contrast the time complexity of running a single-pass reverse topological backward pass versus an iterative relaxation algorithm (such as Bellman-Ford) on a project network with $V$ activities and $E$ relationships.

### Question 2: Mathematical Derivation of Start-to-Start ($SS$) and Start-to-Finish ($SF$) Backward Pass Bounds
In standard literature, Finish-to-Start ($FS$) and Finish-to-Finish ($FF$) relationships directly constrain the predecessor's Late Finish ($LF_i$). However, Start-to-Start ($SS$) and Start-to-Finish ($SF$) relationships originate from the predecessor's start date ($LS_i$).
1. Using first principles of precedence inequalities, write the relationship equation that successor $j$ imposes on predecessor $i$ for an $SS$ relationship with lag $L_{ij}$.
2. Perform the step-by-step algebraic substitution using the relationship $LS_i = LF_i - D_i$ to derive the resulting bound on $LF_i$.
3. Repeat the derivation for a Start-to-Finish ($SF$) relationship with lag $L_{ij}$. Explain why $SF$ is extraordinarily rare in industrial EPC construction.
