### Question 11: 72-Hour Performance Trial Run & Provisional Acceptance
Under the SECI PPA and EPC contract, Commercial Operation Date (COD) requires a successful 72-hour continuous trial run demonstrating a Performance Ratio ($PR \ge 79.5\%$).
$$\text{Performance Ratio } (PR) = \frac{\text{Actual Energy Generated (kWh)}}{\text{Calculated Nominal Energy (kWh)}} = \frac{E_{\text{actual}}}{P_{\text{stc}} \times \left( \frac{H_{\text{poa}}}{G_{\text{stc}}} \right)}$$
Where:
- $E_{\text{actual}}$ = Total AC energy exported to the grid (kWh).
- $P_{\text{stc}} = 140,000\text{ kWp}$ (Installed DC capacity at STC: $1000\text{ W/m}^2$, $25^\circ\text{C}$).
- $H_{\text{poa}}$ = Cumulative Plane-of-Array solar insolation measured by calibrated pyranometers during the 72 hours ($\text{kWh/m}^2$).
- $G_{\text{stc}} = 1.0\text{ kW/m}^2$ (Standard Test Condition irradiance).
- During the 72-hour trial run, the site pyranometers record a cumulative solar insolation of $H_{\text{poa}} = 21.6\text{ kWh/m}^2$.
- The export energy meter at the 220kV delivery point records $2,450,000\text{ kWh}$.
1. Calculate the Calculated Nominal Energy ($E_{\text{nominal}}$) in kWh.
2. Calculate the actual Performance Ratio ($PR$) achieved during the 72-hour trial run to two decimal places.
3. Does the plant meet the contractual threshold ($PR \ge 79.5\%$)? What contractual remedies occur if $PR$ is below the threshold?
